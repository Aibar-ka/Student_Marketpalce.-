## 1. Project / Idea Description

**Project name:** Student Marketplace

**Problem.** University students routinely buy and sell secondhand textbooks, gadgets, furniture, clothing, and other belongings on Telegram or WhatsApp chat, or through general trading platforms. Since the specified channels are not created for college students, it’s tough to find relevant offers, the listings can get lost in conversation threads, and there is no organized way to search through and manage student-to-student sales.

**Proposed solution.** Student Marketplace is a web-based platform specifically designed for university students to buy and sell various goods to each other. Users will be able to create product listings with pictures, descriptions, pricing, and categories, check items that other users posted, search/filter for posted goods, and contact sellers. The platform will provide simplicity in interface and ease in student-to-student communication.

**Target audience.** University students who are looking to purchase or sell secondhand books, electronics, furniture, clothes, and other belongings.

**Expected deliverable (MVP).** A functioning web prototype that consists of student registration and login, product listing creation, category definition, searching and filtering capabilities, product information pages, seller contacts, and basic listing management.

**Alignment with course goals.** The project offers the chance to implement project initiation, project scope definition, WBS, RACI, stakeholder management, prioritization of features, and project management throughout the semester. The initial scope is limited to the MVP in order to keep the time, cost and scope constraints in hand.
